Privacy Policy for Flight Reward Filter

Effective Date: 27 March 2026

1. Overview

Flight Reward Filter is a browser extension designed to filter airline results on the Qantas International Reward Flight Finder entirely on your local device. We value your privacy and aim to be 100% transparent about how the extension operates.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • Local Processing: The extension processes website content entirely on your local machine. No browsing history, website data, or filtered content is ever transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • Local Preferences: Your filter choices (a list of airline codes such as "EK") are saved locally using chrome.storage.local so your settings persist between browser sessions. This data never leaves your device.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, or IP address.

3. Third-Party Services (Donations)

If you choose to support the development of this extension via the "Donation" link, you will be redirected to Stripe, a third-party payment processor.

  • The developer does not have access to your full financial information (credit card numbers, CVV, etc.).
  • Stripe's use of your personal information is governed by their own Privacy Policy.
  • The developer may receive your email address and the amount of the donation for the sole purpose of accounting and acknowledging your support.

4. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the version number of the extension and this document.
